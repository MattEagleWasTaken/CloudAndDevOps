# Why is cloud computing important?
## Summary of the key points highlighted in this class
- Highly scalable and manageable if set up with the right expertise. However, if insufficient thought is given to the setup, structure and the company’s needs, it can quickly become a cost trap.   
- Delegation of responsibilities (redundancies, uptime, security (to some extent))  
- Relatively straightforward deployment with short SLAs

# What are the advantages and disadvantages of the cloud computing model?
## When would you suggest a company to use cloud computing and when would you suggest to do something else?
**Pros of the cloud:**  
- When scalability is likely to play a major role (e.g. in rapidly growing sectors such as AI or similar)  
- When there are regularly many varying demands for different, computationally intensive tasks.  
- Once a company reaches a certain size, the built-in redundancies and ease of management are likely to become a major advantage
**Against the Cloud:**
- Small businesses 
- Predictable tasks that do not need to meet constantly changing demands.
- Where a larger infrastructure is already in place.
- Where certain laws restrict or prohibit its use due to data requiring special protection, etc.
